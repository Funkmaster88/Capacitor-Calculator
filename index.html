📋 How to Measure

⚡ Amps

Set clamp meter to AC Amps. Clamp around one capacitor lead wire while the unit is running.

🔌 Voltage

Set multimeter to AC Volts. Touch probes to both capacitor terminals while the unit is running.

🏷️ Rated µF

Read the µF or MFD value printed on the capacitor label. This is your target rated value.
